# Complete self-study program production brief

## Outcome

Create original, free AI-generated self-study lessons based on a real college's publicly documented degree curriculum. Publish individual completed course PDFs and, only after every course is complete, a full-program download in the existing searchable library.

This is non-accredited self-taught learning, not formal education, an official degree, university courseware, or university-endorsed instruction. Reading the materials does not guarantee mastery, credit, certification, or equivalence to university instruction and assessment.

## Inputs still needed

- First degree to cover.
- College whose published curriculum should be the reference.

## Source discovery

An official, current example is Arizona State University's Business (Business Administration), BA, 2026–2027 curriculum checksheet:
https://degrees.asu.edu/checksheet/2026/CBA/BABUSBABA/null

The source specifies 120 minimum credit hours and separate core, concentration, general-study, and elective requirements. It is an example, not a selected program. A short list of business courses is not a complete curriculum.

## Production sequence

1. Preserve the existing operating prompt verbatim. Treat its sequential lesson process as the production order; the requested offline collection allows lessons to be prepared consecutively instead of waiting for a reader's YES between pages.
2. Research the selected catalog year. Record source URLs, prerequisites, requirements, named elective selections, and a semester-by-semester course map. Label the semester order as a self-study arrangement if the college does not publish one.
3. Account for every requirement. Identify internships, labs, practical experiences, and assessed work that text alone cannot replace. The operating instructions omit internships and written assignments; record those omissions prominently rather than pretending the adapted collection satisfies the university's degree requirements.
4. Create each course's introduction, objectives, modules, and full lesson list before drafting lessons.
5. Draft every lesson separately: lesson outline, prior-material recap where applicable, full explanations, definitions, worked examples, applications, summaries, and next lesson. Do not substitute a syllabus, summary, or promotional text for teaching.
6. Verify relevant YouTube/resource links with live research and generate the required actual visual aids. Do not invent sources, images, institutional syllabi, or claims of completion.
7. Review factual accuracy and coverage against the course outline and source curriculum. Mark review status honestly; AI review is not a substitute for a qualified subject expert.
8. Produce branded, readable PDFs with contents, bookmarks where supported, source citations, linked resources, embedded images, and prominent non-accreditation/non-affiliation notices. If a completion memento is included, label it a self-study completion record, never an official university degree or accredited certificate.
9. Visually inspect every generated page and check links and text extraction. Publish each course only when all its planned lessons are present. Do not publish an empty or partial file as a complete course.
10. Ship fixed PDFs from same-origin public/downloads paths, register descriptive subjects and tags in the existing catalog, and verify real browser downloads. Offer the whole program only once every mapped course is complete.

## Scope and costs

A whole degree requires many independently generated and reviewed lessons, images, and resource checks. It cannot honestly be completed as one model response. Generate and review it in course-sized installments; no work continues automatically after a chat turn ends. Generation consumes workspace credits, so avoid launching a large unattended pipeline without a defined scope.
